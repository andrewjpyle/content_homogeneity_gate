Juniper Flats After Dark: Stargazing Rules, Seasons, and Where to Park

Juniper Flats is a designated dark-sky town, which means the streetlights are amber and shielded and the main street dims at 10pm. The result is a Milky Way visible from the parking lot of the grocery store.

The public observatory on Mesa Road opens its 16-inch telescope on Friday and Saturday nights from May through September. Reservations open on the first of each month and the summer weekends fill within a day.

Bring a red flashlight; white lights are discouraged anywhere on the observatory grounds, and volunteers will hand you a red filter if you forget.

Winter nights are clearer but drop below freezing, and the gravel road to the overlook closes after the first snow.
