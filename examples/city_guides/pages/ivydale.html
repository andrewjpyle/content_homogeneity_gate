Ivydale for Cyclists: Where the Valley Climbs Start and Which Ones to Skip

Ivydale sits at the bottom of three road climbs, and locals rate them by gradient, not by name. Pike Lane averages 9 percent for 1.4 miles and is the one everyone talks about. Saddle Road is longer but gentler and better on a loaded touring bike.

The town has one bike shop, Spoke and Gear on Mill Row, and it closes on Mondays. Carry a spare tube; the nearest alternative is 22 miles away in Corbin.

After the climb, the Ivydale Station cafe in the restored signal box serves a fixed cyclist plate of eggs, beans, and sourdough until 2pm.

Avoid the valley road on market Thursdays, when livestock trailers fill both lanes between 6am and 9am.
