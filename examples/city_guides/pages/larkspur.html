Larkspur on a Budget: Free Museums, Cheap Eats, and the Tuesday Pass

Larkspur's three municipal museums are free on the first Sunday of the month, and the textile museum is free every day for anyone under 18. The other two charge a modest fee the rest of the time.

The Tuesday transit pass covers unlimited tram rides plus half-price entry to the botanic glasshouse, which makes Tuesday the cheapest day to see the town.

Eat at the university canteen on Orchard Street; it is open to the public, takes cards, and the daily stew costs less than a coffee downtown.

The old flour mill hosts a free folk session every Wednesday night. Arrive by 7:30pm if you want a seat on the bench by the wheel.
