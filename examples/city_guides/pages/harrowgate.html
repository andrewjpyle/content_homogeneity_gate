Harrowgate in a Weekend: Canal Locks, Rope Works, and a Two-Hour Ferry

Harrowgate grew up around the six locks of the Tamsin Canal, and the best introduction is the towpath walk from Lock 1 to Lock 6, about 3.2 miles, flat, with a lock keeper's cottage at Lock 4 that sells tea from a hatch on weekends.

The old rope works on Cable Street is now a 400-metre covered market. Go before 10am on Saturday for the smoked eel stall; by noon the queue reaches the far door.

For an afternoon, take the Harrowgate to Marrow Isle ferry. The crossing runs every two hours from April to October, the last return leaves at 6:15pm, and bikes ride free on the lower deck.

Skip the waterfront chain hotels. The four rooms above the Lockside Bakery cost about a third less and the bread arrives at your door at 7am.
